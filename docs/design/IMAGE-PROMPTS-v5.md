# Kreatif Atelier: "One Take Across the World" (ChatGPT image pack v5)

Goes with WORLD-PLAN.md. Replaces IMAGE-PROMPTS-v4.

How to use:
1. Open ONE new ChatGPT chat. Paste the SETUP message and wait for "Ready".
2. Paste the prompts one at a time, in order. Save each image with the file name shown into design-refs/ in your project (keep the folders: home/, pages/, graphics/, plates/, mobile/).
3. If an image drifts: "Redo this. Keep everything from the SETUP message."
4. If text is garbled: "Redo with all quoted text spelled exactly."
5. Sections A, B, E are reference frames (with the website's text and UI). Sections C and D are building materials (graphics and clean environment plates, no UI). Do A and B first.
6. If the chat gets slow or starts forgetting the style (after about 30 images), open a new chat, paste SETUP again and continue.

---

## SETUP (paste first)

```
I'm going to send you prompts, one at a time, for reference images of ONE website. Every image must follow these rules for the whole conversation.

BRAND: Kreatif Atelier, a creative holding company in Hyderabad, with five companies: Aurelius, Golden Guardian Productions, Valora, Rudra Yugen, Asraya. No logos, crests, monograms or brand marks anywhere, ever.

CONCEPT, "One Take Across the World": every image is a frame from ONE uncut camera move across a vast, dense, breathtaking natural world, like a nature documentary crossed with a fantasy film. A golden idea (a folded ribbon of luminous gold light, never a sphere or blob) floats just ahead of the camera, leading it. The journey: monsoon jungle, up through the canopy, above the clouds, five mountain peaks, a desert canyon (Aurelius), a cave of stories (Golden Guardian Productions), a coral coast and reef (Valora), a jade mountain lake (Asraya), a glacier and aurora (Rudra Yugen), a summit, the night sky, and back to the jungle at dawn.

LOOK: photoreal, cinematic CG at the level of a high-end film or game cinematic. DENSE environments with strong foreground, midground and background layers, so the camera feels like it is flying THROUGH the world (leaves, mist, rock, coral, ice brushing past the lens, softly out of focus). Volumetric light, atmospheric haze, shallow depth of field with gaussian blur, subtle film grain. One dominant light per frame. Life where it fits: birds, insects, fish, drifting spores. No people, faces or hands.

CAMERA: the same camera in every frame. Wide cinematic lens, smooth gliding drone/steadicam movement, a touch of directional motion blur at the edges, the focal subject sharp. Every frame shows where the camera is heading next.

COLOUR: each company's land is dominated by its colour: Aurelius indigo (desert dusk), Golden Guardian Productions magenta (cave), Valora coral (sunset and reef), Asraya jade (mist and lake), Rudra Yugen cyan (ice and aurora). Gold (around #C9A45C) is the brand accent and the colour of the idea and the route. One vivid colour per frame.

TEXT INSIDE THE WORLD: words are part of the scene, not stuck on top. They can be carved into rock, written in mist or light, reflected in water, frozen in ice, set as huge type behind objects, or shown as thin callout lines from an object to a label (like a product spec diagram). Text is large, calm and readable. Headlines in a medium-weight geometric sans (like Outfit); narration in an elegant italic serif (like Cormorant Garamond); small labels in a monospace (like IBM Plex Mono), used sparingly.

WEBSITE UI (only when I say "with UI"): top bar with "KREATIF ATELIER" at top-left and "Index" and "Sound" at top-right; a thin act bar along the bottom with the chapter name and a time; a tiny gold journey-trail line in the bottom-left corner showing progress. Nothing else.

AVOID: logos, fake dashboards, readouts, stats or numbers that I didn't give you, tiny pills and badges, boxes inside boxes, frosted-glass panels, purple-blue AI gradients, rainbow edges, floating spheres, stock-photo look, long dashes, lorem ipsum.

FORMAT: landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024) unless I say PHONE (portrait 2:3, 1024 x 1536) or SQUARE.

Render every quoted piece of text exactly as written. Reply only "Ready".
```
