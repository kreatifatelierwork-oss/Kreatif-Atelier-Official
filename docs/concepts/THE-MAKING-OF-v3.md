## 1. The idea in two sentences
**One continuous shot follows a single gold Idea through a world that is still being made. Every place starts as a
drawing of light and becomes real while you work in it, so the film you watch and the world you travel through are
the same thing. When the last place is built, you rise above it and see that the whole world was the legacy.**

**Name:** *The Making of a Legacy: Shot on Location* (PROPOSED subtitle; the film's title stays approved).

What this answers:
- **The story is bigger than the org chart.** The journey is how an idea becomes a legacy:
  1. what Kreatif Atelier believes;
  2. how it works (its five process steps);
  3. what it makes possible (Impact).
  The five verticals are the places the Idea must pass through to become real. The holding company is the reveal at
  the end, not the premise.
- **Not a solar system, and not posters.** You travel *through* places joined by passages, doorways, reflections
  and projections. You never zoom at planets, and you never scroll a stack of pinned slides.
- **"The making of" is literal.** Each place is a film set under construction. Doing that place's action (looking,
  rolling, drawing, breathing, tuning) is what builds it. Your interaction is the plot, not a decoration on it.

## 3. The journey, from the first second to the last
For every place this section gives:
- **what you see;**
- **what you do** (the interactive things in the scene, and "Enter" where there is more to read);
- **the content shown** (word for word from the content files; captions are real HTML set *beside* the subject,
  never on it);
- **how you move to the next place.**

### 00 · The Gate: the opening (buttermax rhythm, beat for beat)
- **See:**
  1. Deep ink. Liquid gold dots drift together and pool into the ✦. It is our glyph built from metaballs, not a
     mark.
  2. The gate appears beneath it.
- **Do:** choose **"Enter with sound"** or **"Enter in silence"** (approved).
- **Then, in one breath:**
  1. A short dark dip.
  2. The **gold halftone flood** (gold rim only, C8) opens the screen into the luminous Atrium, while the frame zooms
     forward and straightens.
  3. **The Idea rises** from a well of light in the Atrium floor.
  4. Last, the **hand-written word** writes itself: *Atelier* in Cormorant italic, on its own line under
     "Kreatif Atelier", so no line mixes type families (see §8).
- **Score:** if chosen, the generated score enters with the flood.
- **Timings:** as measured on buttermax and already built at `/film`. The flood is clean by ~0.75 s, the rise
  starts at +0.72 s and the word writes on from +1.07 s.

## 4. Navigation
- **Forwards and back:**
  - scroll, swipe or drag moves you along the route through every passage, in both directions;
  - the arrow keys step place by place; J, K and L work like an editor's shuttle;
  - **Watch** travels by itself and holds in each place. Any input hands control back to you (**Direct**).
  - the browser's Back button returns to the previous place.
- **Knowing where you are:**
  - the **act bar** (bottom) shows the act and the place, e.g. "Act II · Strategy · The Drafting Hall", plus the
    real film time (minutes:seconds along the route);
  - it has one tick per place; the current tick is gold;
  - the top bar shows "Kreatif Atelier" (text), the sound toggle and **Index**.
  - Nothing else: no edge labels and no readouts (C2, C4).
- **Jumping (one or two clicks):**
  - **One click:** any tick on the act bar flies you there through the passages at speed (about 1.2 s, never a hard
    cut).
  - **Two clicks:** **Index** opens a **floor plan of the Atelier** (from the House of Light). It shows every place,
    its sub-rooms (each service, each founder, Formats, Contact) and a "you are here" dot. Choose one and you go
    there.
  - Every place and room has its own link (e.g. `/#aurelius/brand-strategy-identity`), so it can be shared or
    bookmarked.
- **Going deeper and getting back out:**
  - **Enter** (PROPOSED label) on a thing in a place moves the camera into a small room off that place: a service,
    an About panel, a founder or a format;
  - the full text sits there as a readable HTML panel beside the subject;
  - **Back** (button, Esc, or the browser's Back) returns you to the same spot you left, with the place unchanged.

## 5. Signature moments
1. **The opening.** The ✦ pools from liquid gold, you choose sound, gold floods the screen open, the Idea rises,
   and *Atelier* writes itself.
2. **Seeing through the drawing.** In the Drafting Hall the ink shows the built world under the sketch. Then you
   find the angle, and the whole hall builds itself around you.
3. **Walking through the projection.** In the Projection Hall the image you rolled becomes the next place, and you
   step into the picture.
4. **Diving through the reflection.** After three breaths the pool goes still, and you fall through your own
   reflection into a lattice of glass.
5. **The view from the Roof.** You rise above everything you built: five worlds around one centre, joined by your
   route, while the Idea turns to solid gold. Only here does the visitor see the holding company whole.

## 6. The cursor, place by place
The base everywhere is the rebuilt buttermax ink: one liquid blob that trails your pointer, inverts what it covers,
slides on after you stop, fades in about a second and has a thin gold edge. It is on by default and always on top of
the page. On top of that base, each place gives the ink one job:

| Place | What the ink does there |
|---|---|
| Atrium, Studio Floor | The pure base: it inverts, and its flow stirs the Idea's light |
| Drafting Hall | **The Lens:** what it covers shows the built hall under the drawing |
| Projection Hall | **A focus brush:** what it covers comes sharp |
| Concourse | **A current:** its flow steers the gold streams |
| Water Court | **The path:** where it passes over water, stepping stones rise |
| Garden Court | **Calm:** slower and softer, it spreads as ripples |
| Glass Lattice | **A signal:** panes it touches turn and light up |
| Roof | Inverts the view and lights the route line under it |
| Gallery, Post-credits | Plain inversion over the credits. At the end it gathers to the ✦ |

The system cursor always stays visible, so the ink is an effect, not a replacement cursor.

## 8. The look (C1–C8, `DESIGN.md`)
- **Light by default ("Luminous"):**
  - neutral, bright, pale plaster and stone (canvas `#F4F5F7`), never cream-and-brass;
  - inside a vertical's place, its colour is the light, the atmosphere and the tint of the materials (vivid and
    dominant, C5);
  - outside the verticals, gold is the only accent for buttons, links, the active act and the ✦.
- **Dark alternate ("Cinema"):** the same places at night. Deep ink surfaces, where each place's own coloured light
  is the only light.
- **One light per place (C7):** listed in §2. No halos, no bloom on everything.
- **Edges (C8):** the flood rim and the ink's edge are gold only.
- **Type (C6), three roles, never mixed in one line:**
  - Outfit for display;
  - Cormorant Garamond italic for voice lines and the hand-written *Atelier*, which sits on its own line;
  - IBM Plex Mono for the act bar and small labels.
- **Captions:** each sits *beside* its subject in one off-grid block. Nothing is printed on the imagery.
- **Shapes:** sculptures and forms are original, abstract and never read as logos or as the chart's icons.

## 9. Phones and reduced motion
- **Phones (portrait):**
  - every place has its own portrait framing, with the subject in the upper two-thirds and the caption below;
  - a vertical swipe travels;
  - the ink follows your finger (the rebuilt touch ink is already as smooth as the mouse);
  - each interaction has a touch version: hold for Roll and Breathe, tap for Launch, Spark and Lay a stone, drag
    for Find the angle (or step buttons);
  - "Enter" rooms open as bottom sheets, and the Index floor plan fills the screen;
  - DPR ≤ 1.5, no depth of field, simpler glass and water, and places stream in behind the passages.
- **Reduced motion:**
  - places change by **cut + crossfade** instead of camera travel; the haze is a still tint;
  - the Idea and the sculptures are still;
  - the ink is faint, small and slow;
  - holds become presses; Watch advances place by place with crossfades;
  - every word, the act bar, the Index and the Enter rooms work exactly the same.
- **No WebGL / weak devices:** a CSS version of the same places, with the reference images as stills, the same text
  and the same navigation.

## 11. Honest risks
- **Scope.** Ten bespoke places is the largest build of all the proposals. It is built in phases (§12), each
  shippable, and every place is simple geometry, so the cost goes into light and craft rather than models.
- **Lighting craft.** Architecture only looks expensive with convincing light: baked soft shadows, volumetric
  shafts and good glass. If a place doesn't reach the reference image, it doesn't ship.
- **Phones.** Water, glass and coloured fog are costly. Places stream in behind passages, and phones get simpler
  materials. The mobile Performance ≥ 70 target must be re-proven at each phase (today `/film` scores 93).
- **The "being built" effect must read in one glance.** Line drawing turning into a solid place is the whole idea.
  If testers miss it, it fails. It will be the first thing tested with the reference images.
- **Content density.** Service texts are long. They live in Enter rooms, so places stay calm, but some visitors
  never enter. The Index and the Gallery credits list everything by name as a safety net.
- **Interaction friction.** Find the angle and Draw the bridge can feel fiddly. Each has a generous snap, a time
  limit after which it completes itself, and a one-press keyboard and touch version.
- **The holding-company reveal arrives late.** Skimmers may miss it. The Index lists the five verticals from the
  first second, and Watch reaches the Roof in about 8 minutes.
- **Copy that contradicts the chart** (the "four divisions" lines) must be decided before build (DECISIONS §2).
- **Sound** is a craft effort of its own (one piece with a voice per place). It stays silent until chosen, so the
  site never depends on it.
