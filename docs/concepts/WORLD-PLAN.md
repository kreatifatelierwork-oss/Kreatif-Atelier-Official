# Kreatif Atelier: "One Take Across the World" (world plan v5)

Replaces the "Shot on Location" room-by-room look. Keeps the locked navigation engine (its feel: smoothing, settling, scroll/swipe/keys/Watch) and the locked cursor ink exactly as they are. Only the world, the route, the text system and the page structure change.

## The idea in two sentences
A single golden idea is carried across the whole world in one uncut camera move: from the floor of a monsoon jungle, up through the canopy, above the clouds and across five lands, each land being one of Kreatif Atelier's companies. It feels like a nature film crossed with a fantasy epic, and every frame is also an information page, because the words live inside the world.

## What changes, and why
| Problem now | Fix |
|---|---|
| Bare rooms, no environments | Seven dense, cinematic natural worlds: jungle, sky, desert canyon, cave, coast and reef, mountain lake, glacier, summit and night sky. Foreground, midground and background layers so the camera flies THROUGH things (leaves, mist, rock arches, coral, ice) not past flat walls. |
| Camera moves between rooms | One continuous flight path with transitions that are physical: up a tree, through the canopy, down a canyon, into a cave, underwater, through ice. Never a fade between scenes. |
| Text flies in late and leaves fast | Text is anchored in the 3D world. It appears as its place approaches, moves with parallax as the camera passes, and stays until the place is behind you. Lifetime is tied to camera position, never to timers. |
| Not enough information | Text at every point of the route. Something is always readable: a chapter title, a line of narration, a callout, a graphic. |
| Text all looks the same | Seven ways of presenting text (below), including turning content into graphics: maps, constellations, strata, route diagrams, elevation profiles, field-guide pages, film title cards. |
| Navigation too short | Home journey about 6 to 8 minutes in Watch mode across 12 chapters. Each company page is its own 3 to 5 minute journey. |
| Companies only visited in passing | Five separate pages, one per company, entered through a portal with a world transition, each a full journey through that company's land. |

## The seven ways text appears
1. **Embedded text:** carved into canyon rock, written in mist, reflected in water, frozen in ice, glowing on leaves. Used for names and taglines.
2. **Anchored narration:** lines of copy floating in 3D space beside the path, moving with parallax as you fly past. Used for paragraphs.
3. **Callouts (Oryzo style):** thin leader lines from an object in the world to a label and short text. Used for the four offerings of each company and for service features.
4. **Giant background type:** huge words set behind a subject, partly hidden by foreground (e.g. "STORIES" behind a waterfall).
5. **Graphical text:** content turned into diagrams that belong to the world (see the list below).
6. **Film title cards:** chapter openings set like a film's titles, with thin letterbox bars sliding in.
7. **Field-guide pages:** an explorer's journal page that unfolds over the scene for dense content (service details, pricing), then folds away.

- **"5 Verticals" and "∞ Creative Vision":** giant numerals formed by light through the canopy.
- **The five pillars:** five mountain peaks rising from the clouds, each named on a topographic label.
- **The five companies:** an atlas/route map drawn over the sky showing the five lands and the gold route.
- **Engagement models:** three terraces/strata of the canyon, like a geological cross-section, with the exact prices.
- **How We Work (5 steps):** an elevation-profile trail line with five waypoints.
- **Industries (13):** a star chart where the 13 industries are stars in one constellation.
- **Our ecosystem serves (6):** six trails converging on the summit, drawn like a route network.
- **Impact (6):** six beams/markers around the gold monument.
- **Founders:** film credits rolling over the night sky, with empty portrait frames ready for photos.
- **Location:** Hyderabad's coordinates and a contour map in the closing scene.

## Overlays that come and go on scroll
Thin letterbox bars and title cards at chapter starts; a small journey trail (the route so far, real progress) in a corner; topographic contour lines and map grids that drift across transitions; soft gold light leaks; subtle film grain. No fake readouts, numbers or system labels.

## COMPANY PAGES (/aurelius, /golden-guardian-productions, /valora, /rudra-yugen, /asraya)
- Entered by clicking a portal (or from the Index). The camera flies THROUGH the portal and the world takes over (world transition). The 3D canvas persists across the page change so there is no blank load.
- Each page: arrival (name, tagline, value line), then one chapter per offering (four chapters, each its own spot in that land with a different text treatment), then a closing with "Enquire" (mailto) and two portals: "Next: [next company]" and "Back to the Atlas".
- **Aurelius** also carries: the agency line, all six services in full (field-guide pages), the engagement models as canyon strata with exact prices, How We Work, industries.
- **Golden Guardian Productions** also carries: the creative production service in full; empty showreel frames ready for real footage. Each offering chamber uses a different kind of moving picture made of light: zoetrope, shadow play, camera obscura, light painting.
- **Valora, Rudra Yugen, Asraya:** the chart has only one line per offering. Claude Code drafts 2 to 3 sentences per offering, descriptive only (no facts, numbers, clients or claims), clearly marked PROPOSED for approval.

## Building approach (for Claude Code to decide details)
Hybrid: real-time 3D for the camera, fog, light, water, particles, the Idea, embedded 3D text and callouts; dense environments built from layered matte-painting plates (from the ChatGPT asset images) arranged at depth for real parallax, plus real 3D foreground elements near the camera (leaves, rocks, coral, ice) that the camera flies through. Depth of field and gaussian blur sell the depth. Assets stream per chapter; compressed textures; a lighter tier for phones; the still fallback stays.

## Locked
The navigation engine and its feel, and the cursor ink, are frozen. New route, new worlds, new pages: same engine, same feel, same ink. Baseline proofs re-run at the end.
